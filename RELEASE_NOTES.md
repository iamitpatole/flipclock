## 🌟 Product Overview
**FlipClock** is a premium, beautifully crafted Flutter application inspired by classic mechanical desk clocks. Combining retro aesthetics with modern utility, it features realistic 3D-perspective flip animations and a suite of customizable tools, making it the perfect desk companion, nightstand clock, or fullscreen screensaver.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🕒 Realistic Mechanical Animations
*   **Custom Flip Cards:** Features top and bottom card rotation that mimics the physical sensation of mechanical flip clocks.
*   **Animate Seconds:** Toggleable seconds display with active mechanical card animations.

### 🎨 Visual & Appearance Customizations
*   **6 Built-in Themes:**
    *   *Classic Black:* The traditional retro desk clock look.
    *   *Retro Brown:* Cozy mechanical aesthetics with warm brown tones.
    *   *OLED:* Pure black background optimized for battery saving.
    *   *Blue Neon:* A sleek, modern glowing-cyberpunk design.
    *   *Green Terminal:* Command-line retro style with glowing green digits.
    *   *Minimal White:* Clean, high-contrast minimalist look.
*   **Dynamic Accent Colors:** Choose from custom swatches (Sky Blue, Warm Amber, Emerald Green, Electric Red, Pastel Purple, Slate White).
*   **Adjustable Scale:** Slider to scale digits up or down to suit any screen size.
*   **Animation Speed Controls:** Custom control slider to adjust card flipping speeds.

### ⚙️ Behavior & Screensaver Options
*   **Fullscreen Mode:** Easily hide system and navigation bars to transform your device into a dedicated desk screensaver.
*   **Keep Screen Awake:** Option to prevent the display from sleeping or dimming during use.
*   **Time & Date Formats:** Toggle 12-hour/24-hour clock formats and display the current date and day of the week.

### 💧 Smart Health & Utility Integrations
*   **Water Drink Reminder:** Set custom recurring alerts (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, or 4 hours) directly within the app to stay hydrated throughout the day.
*   **Live Battery Indicator:** Keep track of your device's power levels and charging state in real-time, beautifully integrated into the clock layout.
